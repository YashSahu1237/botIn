Leave Fine POC — Build Checklist

One concern, end to end, in ~3 weeks. The goal is to answer five questions that would be expensive to get wrong later — not to build a small version of everything.

0 of 56 done
What this POC proves. (1) Flowable really pauses and resumes across a restart. (2) The LLM hits usable accuracy on real Hinglish. (3) REQUIRES_NEW protects the money record through a rollback. (4) Whether the leave/fine APIs exist in empapi. (5) Whether a DMN edit changes behaviour without a redeploy. Everything else in the design is conventional Spring Boot work.

Excluded on purpose: telephony, the SP mobile app, the agent screen, observability, access control, guardrails, and 29 of the 30 concerns. The hosting decision (standalone vs inside empapi) stays deferred — build standalone.
