Two things in this mapping invalidate earlier plans.

Fine Related is deprecated. 12,781 of June volume — the single largest bucket — has no standalone flow: automated fines now link to the strike system only, and disputes route to Violations. The Leave Fine POC is built on a concern that no longer exists. With Saving <Rs5000 also retired, 13,823 tickets (37% of stated volume) leave the build entirely.

Violations is now the centre of gravity. 17 of 38 concerns and 54 of 113 rules — nearly half the engine — and it carries no volume figure at all. It is also the only domain with a defined verdict vocabulary (accept / uphold / ticket) and the only one where the bot changes state without touching money.

1Outcome vocabulary, mapped to tiers

The mapping uses eight outcome types. Our tier model has four. They do not line up one-to-one, and two of the mismatches matter.

Rule counts by outcome type. Tier assignment is our mapping, not stated in the source.
Outcome typeTierRulesWhat it means
SELF-SERVET04Deflect to the app. No ticket, no agent.

REROUTE has no equivalent in the current design. 14 rules — one in eight — end by handing the session to a different concern's flow rather than resolving it. Type 5 (Job Reject) is almost entirely reroute: six of its eight rules just dispatch to R1, R7, R9, R10, R8 or R3. In BPMN this is a Call Activity or a re-entry into concern selection; today's design has neither, and a process that ends by starting another needs a defined ticket and session identity story.

UPHOLD is a distinct outcome, not a T1 answer. 17 rules reject a challenge. It behaves like T1 — bot replies, no state change — but it is a denial of an appeal, which is exactly the case most likely to produce a dissatisfied partner and fire agent-connect trigger A. Worth its own template class and its own CSAT watch.

Three counters are stateful across time, not per-request. The pooled emergency counter, both 25-job-cycle allowances, and the monthly withdrawal counts all need a durable per-SP tally with a defined reset boundary. The Leave Fine POC no longer has a subject. Four concerns, sixteen rules, cover every outcome type in the build and exercise every mechanism the architecture rests on.

Why Transport rather than another money concern: it is the only one whose decision needs no language understanding, which lets the money path be proven independently of classifier accuracy. That separates the two hardest unknowns instead of testing them together — if the POC fails, you know which half failed.

Together these four cover BOT-inform, BOT-act, UPHOLD, TICKET, REROUTE and SELF-SERVE — every outcome type except the six rules already marked out of build.
